# Worker arrangements inventory (issue #335)

**Status: evidence-backed technical inventory; worker-owner and Romanian payroll/accounting review pending.** This document describes repository structures and workflows. It does not classify any real person for legal or tax purposes and does not establish that production records are complete.

## Scope and evidence standard

The repository was inspected for worker, contract, activity, payslip, supplier invoice, transaction, permission, audit, and workflow structures. The available source establishes what the application can represent, not which arrangements are actually used by the business. No production roster, signed contracts, vendor master export, or owner-approved worker classifications were available for this inventory. Accordingly, counts of employees, PFA contractors, other individual contractors, and legal-entity suppliers are **unknown**, not zero.

## Source-of-truth and lifecycle map

1. **Employee identity:** employee record and linked application user are separate records. The business owner must confirm the authoritative HR roster and reconcile it to both records.
2. **Employee terms:** employee contract and its effective dates/rates/clauses are the available application representation. Signed contract and amendments must remain the legal source documents; the repository does not prove their completeness.
3. **Employee activity:** timesheets, sessions and other approved activity sources feed source-linked payslip lines. Each payable source type and its approver must be confirmed by the worker/finance owner.
4. **Employee payslip:** generation creates an Open period record and source snapshots. Closure and payment are lifecycle states; they must not be treated as proof of bank settlement without supporting evidence. Corrections should preserve the original and use an audited adjustment or approved correcting record.
5. **Supplier/PFA candidate:** vendor and vendor-contract records are supplier administration. Vendor invoices link to contracts and may be linked to transactions/allocations. A qualified owner must classify the underlying contracting party before any payroll or tax treatment is applied.
6. **Corrections and reversals:** preserve the original contract, invoice, payslip, activity and transaction history. Record reason, source evidence, authorizer, effective period, and links to the corrected/reversal record. Do not silently rewrite historical snapshots or reinterpret legacy amounts.

## Access, audit and retention observations

- Employee, employee-contract, timesheet, vendor, vendor-contract, vendor-invoice and transaction models use activity logging for selected fields; several also support soft deletion. Logging configuration varies by model and does not by itself prove a complete audit trail or access policy.
- Payslip line items explicitly reject update and delete operations; closed/paid payslips are guarded by model-level lifecycle rules. These protections should be preserved.
- The exact production roles, access grants, separation of duties, retention schedule, backup restoration process and operator procedures are not verifiable from these models alone. Review `docs/authorization.md`, `docs/audit-logging.md`, `docs/employee-payslip-workflow.md`, and `docs/finance-payroll-readiness.md` with the responsible owners.
- This inventory contains no personal data or production worker counts.
