# Romanian labour and payroll requirements review (issue #336)

**Review date:** 2026-10-25  
**Status:** Official-source research draft; applicability and calculation policy require qualified Romanian payroll/legal/accounting review. This is not legal or tax advice and is not authorization to implement statutory calculations.

## Scope and dependency

This review follows [the worker arrangements inventory](worker-arrangements-inventory.md). That inventory identifies employee contracts and activity-based payslips in the code, plus generic vendor contracts/invoices, but the actual production worker roster and owner-approved classifications are not available. Therefore, employee requirements below are a preliminary legal-source map. PFA and other engagement requirements remain conditional on owner-confirmed arrangements; vendor records must not be treated as proof of PFA status.

The primary sources consulted are the Romanian Labour Inspectorate's current legislation index and Labour Code guidance, the official Ministry of Justice legislation portal for Law 227/2015 (Fiscal Code), and ANAF material for independent-activity social contributions. Official texts and amendments must be rechecked for each effective period before policy approval.

## Preliminary requirements matrix

| Topic | Source-backed requirement area | Data and controls the application will need to support | Decisions that remain open |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Employment relationship and contract | The Labour Code and Labour Inspectorate guidance describe the written individual employment contract and required terms, including parties, start date, occupation, workplace, schedule, salary/benefits and other applicable terms. The official guidance states the contract is concluded before work begins. | Preserve the signed contract and amendments, effective start/end dates, worker and employer identifiers, occupation, work location, working schedule, salary basis and benefits. Keep source document and reported register data traceable. | Reconcile every actual employee to a signed contract and REGES-ONLINE record; confirm which contract forms and special clauses apply. |
| Employee register | HG 295/2025 and REGES-ONLINE govern the general employee register and reporting of relevant employment-contract events. | Track the source event, effective date, submission time, responsible operator, acknowledgement/errors, correction history and evidence of successful reporting. | Payroll/legal owner must confirm current event-specific deadlines, required fields, correction workflow and operational responsibility. No REGES integration is specified by this document. |
| Pay period and salary basis | Salary terms and payment period are employment-contract matters. Minimum wage and other salary rules depend on the applicable legislation and effective period. | Effective-dated gross salary, full/part-time norm, hourly/monthly basis, salary changes, pay date and the policy version used for each period. | Confirm all real contract types, currencies, minimum-wage applicability and historical changes with the payroll specialist. Do not interpret existing integer legacy columns as approved currency semantics. |
| Working time and overtime | Labour Code guidance covers normal working time, weekly limits, overtime consent/compensation, night work, daily and weekly rest. Activity data currently stored in Management-OS is not by itself an approved legal time record. |
| Leave and absences | Labour Code guidance covers annual leave, public holidays and specified protected/medical leave. Special conditions and amendments may apply. | Leave type, effective dates, source evidence, approval, balances/accrual where applicable, effect on the pay period and correction history, with restricted access to sensitive records. | Verify the actual leave types used, authoritative source, payroll impact and privacy rules. Do not infer a statutory payment formula from existing holiday-hour logic. |
| Salary income tax and social contributions | Fiscal Code, especially its salary-income and mandatory-contribution provisions, supplies the statutory basis. Bases, exemptions, deductions, employer/employee responsibilities and rates can change by period and facts. | Versioned rules with effective dates, explicit taxable bases, eligibility facts, deductions/exemptions, rounding stage, calculation explanation and retained source version. | Qualified Romanian payroll/accounting specialist must approve each applicable rule and representative expected results before implementation. This document deliberately does not publish a calculation rate table. |
| Benefits, allowances and reimbursements | Treatment depends on the legal category, contract/policy terms, statutory limits and tax rules effective for the period. | Distinguish salary, taxable/non-taxable benefits, allowances and expense reimbursements by type; preserve evidence, eligibility, cap and calculation version. | Review actual benefits and the existing own-equipment reimbursement clause. No benefit is tax-exempt merely because it is represented as a reimbursement in JSON. |
| Payroll reporting, payment and corrections | Fiscal Code and applicable ANAF reporting rules govern tax declarations and remittances; the Labour Code and contract govern salary payment obligations. Specific reporting form/version and deadlines must be verified for the period. | Separate calculated amount, approved amount, declared amount, remittance/payment evidence and reconciliation status. No current production filing workflow was established by this repository audit. |
| PFA / independent activity | Fiscal Code and OUG 44/2008 are relevant source areas for authorized independent activity; contribution and declaration treatment differs from salary employment and depends on current rules and the individual's circumstances. | Separate counterparty legal form and fiscal identity, verified registration/evidence, contract, invoice, approval, payment, declarations and policy version. Do not create employee payslips for PFA invoices. | First confirm whether the business actually engages PFA contractors and review each arrangement. Specialist must determine income category, applicable tax/contributions, payer obligations and effective period. |

Existing model activity logging is not proof of complete compliance. |

## Required separation of workflows

- **Employee payroll:** contract and effective-dated employment facts → approved time/activity/leave inputs → specialist-approved calculation policy → reviewed payslip → declaration/remittance/payment evidence → period close and audited corrections.
- **PFA / independent contractor:** verified contractor status and contract → supplier invoice and expense/payment approval → separately reviewed tax/declaration obligations → transaction and bank reconciliation. Do not reuse employee deductions, pay-slip statuses or employee contribution logic by default.
- **Other vendor/legal entity:** supplier invoice and contract lifecycle unless the owner confirms a different legal arrangement. Vendor naming, category or invoice existence is not sufficient evidence to classify an individual as a PFA or employee.

## Policy implementation gates

1. HR/finance provides the real worker-arrangement roster and contract evidence required by issue #335.
2. A qualified Romanian labour/payroll/accounting reviewer maps each actual arrangement to the applicable legal sources and records effective dates and unresolved interpretations.
3. The reviewer supplies signed calculation fixtures for representative normal cases, boundary cases, mid-period changes, absences, benefits, exemptions and corrections, as applicable to the actual workforce.
4. Finance/HR approves the calculation order, input definitions, rounding, versioning, authorization, audit and period-close policy.
5. Only then may the data contract and deterministic engine issues (#337 onward) implement the approved policy. Until then, existing activity-based payslips remain operational records and must not be represented as statutory payroll calculations.
