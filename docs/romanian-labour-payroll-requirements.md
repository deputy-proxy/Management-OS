# Romanian labour and payroll requirements review (issue #336)

**Review date:** 2026-10-25  
**Status:** Official-source research draft; applicability and calculation policy require qualified Romanian payroll/legal/accounting review. This is not legal or tax advice and is not authorization to implement statutory calculations.

## Scope and dependency

This review follows [the worker arrangements inventory](worker-arrangements-inventory.md). That inventory identifies employee contracts and activity-based payslips in the code, plus generic vendor contracts/invoices, but the actual production worker roster and owner-approved classifications are not available. Therefore, employee requirements below are a preliminary legal-source map. PFA and other engagement requirements remain conditional on owner-confirmed arrangements; vendor records must not be treated as proof of PFA status.

The primary sources consulted are the Romanian Labour Inspectorate's current legislation index and Labour Code guidance, the official Ministry of Justice legislation portal for Law 227/2015 (Fiscal Code), and ANAF material for independent-activity social contributions. Official texts and amendments must be rechecked for each effective period before policy approval.

## Required separation of workflows

- **Employee payroll:** contract and effective-dated employment facts → approved time/activity/leave inputs → specialist-approved calculation policy → reviewed payslip → declaration/remittance/payment evidence → period close and audited corrections.
- **PFA / independent contractor:** verified contractor status and contract → supplier invoice and expense/payment approval → separately reviewed tax/declaration obligations → transaction and bank reconciliation. Do not reuse employee deductions, pay-slip statuses or employee contribution logic by default.
- **Other vendor/legal entity:** supplier invoice and contract lifecycle unless the owner confirms a different legal arrangement. Vendor naming, category or invoice existence is not sufficient evidence to classify an individual as a PFA or employee.

## Policy implementation gates

1. HR/finance provides the real worker-arrangement roster and contract evidence required by issue #335.
2. A qualified Romanian labour/payroll/accounting reviewer maps each actual arrangement to the applicable legal sources and records effective dates and unresolved interpretations.
3. The reviewer supplies signed calculation fixtures for representative normal cases, boundary cases, mid-period changes, absences, benefits, exemptions and corrections, as applicable to the actual workforce.
4. Finance/HR approves the calculation order, input definitions, rounding, versioning, authorization, audit and period-close policy.
5. Only then may the data contract and deterministic engine issues (#337 onward) implement the approved policy. Until then, existing activity-based payslips remain operational records and must not be represented as statutory payroll calculations.

### Effective-date and correction contract

Every future policy rule or reporting schema must carry at least: `source_instrument`, `source_url`, `article_or_schema`, `published_or_checked_at`, `effective_from`, `effective_until` (when known), `reviewer_status`, and the immutable policy/schema version attached to the payroll period. A new rule must not silently recalculate or overwrite an approved historical payslip. Recalculation or declaration correction must create a traceable correction/replacement record linked to the original inputs, result, submission and reason.

The source links above are discovery and evidence references, not proof that every provision shown is currently applicable to every possible worker. The reviewer must verify the controlling consolidated text and amendment commencement date for each target payroll period, record the applicable interpretation, and supply approved test vectors before implementation.
